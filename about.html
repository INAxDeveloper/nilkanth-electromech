About Us

Welcome to Nilkanth Electromech

Your premier choice for electrical panel solutions in Surat. With a reputation for excellence, we take pride in being the best manufacturer of L.T. (Low Tension), H.T. (High Tension), and APFC (Automatic Power Factor Correction) panels in the region.

At Nilkanth Electromech, we have built a strong foundation of expertise and experience in the electrical industry. Our team of highly skilled professionals is dedicated to providing top-notch panel building services that meet the highest industry standards. With our commitment to quality, reliability, and innovation, we have established ourselves as a trusted name in the market.

We specialize in designing, manufacturing, and supplying a wide range of electrical panels that cater to various industrial, commercial, and residential needs. Whether you require L.T. panels for distribution purposes, H.T. panels for high voltage applications, or APFC panels for efficient power factor correction, we have the expertise to deliver customized solutions that meet your specific requirements.

What sets us apart is our relentless focus on quality. We use only the finest materials and components in our panel manufacturing process, ensuring durability and long-lasting performance. Our panels undergo rigorous testing to ensure compliance with safety standards, and we strive to exceed our customers' expectations in terms of reliability, efficiency, and functionality.
