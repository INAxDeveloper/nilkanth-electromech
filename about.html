About Us

Welcome to Nilkanth Electromech

Your premier choice for electrical panel solutions in Surat. We take pride in being a "CPRI" Certified Switchboard Manufacturer and Government Approved Licensed Electrical Contractor.

At Nilkanth Electromech, we have built a strong foundation of expertise and experience in the electrical industry. With over 14 years of precision engineering, our team of highly skilled professionals is dedicated to providing top-notch panel building services that meet the highest national and international industry standards.

We specialize in designing, manufacturing, testing, and commissioning a comprehensive range of electrical panels for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure applications. Whether you require L.T. panels for distribution, H.T. panels for high voltage applications, or APFC panels for efficient power factor correction, we deliver custom-engineered solutions with guaranteed reliability.

"CPRI" Certified Switchboard Manufacturer

Type-tested and verified by Central Power Research Institute for short-circuit withstand, IP degree of protection, and temperature rise.

Gov. Approved Licence Electrical Contractor

Authorized and licensed by the Government for High Voltage (H.V.) and Low Voltage (L.V.) electrical installations, cable laying, and commissioning.
