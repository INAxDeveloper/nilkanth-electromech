What We Do

Powering Your Electrical Infrastructure:
CPRI Certified Manufacturing & Services

We provide end-to-end electrical panel services from CPRI compliant fabrication to Gov. licensed installation, commissioning and testing.

Fabrication Work (Control Panel)

We provide CPRI-compliant customised fabrication solutions for control panels, modular enclosures, and industrial switchboards built to rigid engineering tolerances and Indian electrical standards (IS/IEC).

H.V/L.V. Cable Laying

As a Government Approved Licensed Electrical Contractor, our cable laying services cover both High Voltage (HV) and Low Voltage (LV) certified installations, ensuring safe, certified transmission across industrial complexes and commercial setups.

L.T./H.T. Panel Commissioning

Our systematic on-site commissioning guarantees optimal relay coordination, busbar calibration, insulation integrity, and smooth system handover by certified engineers.

L.T./H.T. Panel Installation

Professional on-site mechanical and electrical installation by government licensed technicians ensuring safety, seismic stability, and code compliance across Gujarat.

L.T. Panel Retro Fitting Jobs

Modernizing legacy electrical panels with contemporary switchgear, intelligent breakers, and digital meters, extending equipment lifespan with minimum downtime.

L.T./H.T. Panel Testing Jobs

Thorough insulation resistance, high-voltage withstand, dielectric strength, and functional testing matching CPRI testing standards to prevent operational hazards and downtime.
